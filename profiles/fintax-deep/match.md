# What belongs

Explanations of how crypto-asset accounting, tax reporting, due diligence, self-certification, TIN collection, or filing actually works. In scope: IFRS, IASB, and FASB materials; OECD CARF or CRS mechanics; custody, stablecoin, tokenised RWA, and DeFi treatment when tied to a stated reporting or accounting mechanism.

# What does not belong

Price moves, market wraps, exchange gossip, protocol hype, and items that name a standard without describing a mechanism. Do not include generic crypto news that a timely official-development profile would also reject.
