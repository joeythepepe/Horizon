# Evaluation goal

Decide whether the item explains a mechanism an editor can research for institutional crypto accounting or tax compliance. Candidate research only. Do not draft a finished article.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10:** A primary standards or authority text that states a recognition, measurement, due-diligence, or reporting mechanic.
- **7-8:** A concrete explanation of that mechanic with the source document identifiable in the item.
- **5-6:** A useful technical discussion that stays within the supplied facts.
- **3-4:** A topic mention without a mechanism.
- **0-2:** Price commentary, rumors, or ungrounded product claims.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer the supplied text. Do not browse. Do not upgrade a secondary recap into a statement of the standard. Use three to five tags naming the standard or procedure.
