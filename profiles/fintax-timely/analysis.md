# Evaluation goal

Decide whether the item is a dated official development an editor should assess for institutional crypto tax reporting. Candidate research only. Do not draft a finished article.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10:** A dated official action with a deadline, scope, or enforcement consequence for reporting institutions.
- **7-8:** A concrete official publication or legislative step with a clear compliance effect.
- **5-6:** A credible report that names the official document and date and does not add unsupported claims.
- **3-4:** Related background without a new decision, deadline, or enforcement fact.
- **0-2:** Prices, rumors, undated commentary, or claims about a product's capabilities.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer the supplied text. Do not browse. Do not treat a Google News redirect as the agency's own URL. Use three to five tags naming the regime and the authority, not market sectors.
