# What belongs

Official or directly reported decisions, deadlines, enforcement, legislation, consultations, or technical publications about institutional crypto-asset tax reporting and compliance. In scope: CARF, DAC8, Form 1099-DA, CRS amendments, TIN collection, self-certification, and reporting XML, including publications from OECD, IRS, IRD, IRAS, HMRC, and EU tax authorities.

# What does not belong

Token prices, exchange outages, listings, fundraising, influencer commentary, generic blockchain news, rumors, and product marketing. A recognizable agency name is not enough when the item does not change a reporting duty, deadline, or enforcement position.
